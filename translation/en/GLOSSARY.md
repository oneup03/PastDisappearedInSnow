# English glossary

Names and terms for the English translation, so all three parts agree. Family names
follow given names. Names shared with Nintendo's English Switch releases (*The Missing Heir*,
*The Girl Who Stands Behind*, *Emio*) follow them: Ayumi Tachibana, Shunsuke Utsugi,
"Mr. Utsugi", the Utsugi Detective Agency. Those releases keep Japanese names and drop
honorifics, as this one does. A reading marked (?) is my best guess, with no furigana in the game.

## Voice

Plain, natural American English with the feel of a TV mystery. Short sentences: there's
little room. Family and friends use first names. Drop honorifics, using Mr., Ms., Dr. and
titles where the Japanese is formal. Ayumi is warm, quick and a bit teasing with people she
knows, and polite with strangers. Utsugi is calm and kind. Sakuma is gruff and official.
Villagers speak plainly, in a rural but not comic way.

Parentheses mark Ayumi's thoughts, as in the Japanese. Pauses (・・・) become "...".
Letters and quotes in 『』 become "double quotes". Use straight ASCII quotes and apostrophes.
Numbers as digits for facts of time ("3 years ago", "2 and 3 a.m.", "February 9"). Officers
call Ayumi "Ms. Tachibana". Profiles read "Age 17." with American school years.

## Clues to keep word for word

- "The footprints leaving the annex first head toward the gate, then toward the front door."
- Snow began "in the evening" and stopped "shortly after 1 a.m."
- Estimated time of death: "between 2 and 3 a.m." The body was found "shortly before 6".
