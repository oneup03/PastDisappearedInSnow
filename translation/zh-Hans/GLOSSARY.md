# 简体中文术语表 (Simplified Chinese glossary)

Names and terms for the Simplified Chinese translation, so all three parts agree. It is
translated from the Japanese, with the English pack (`translation/en/`) as a reference for
meaning and for decisions already made there. Names shared with Nintendo's Chinese releases
of the Famicom Detective Club games follow them: 橘步美, 空木俊介, 空木侦探事务所.

Japanese names keep their kanji, in simplified form (橘则子, 草野刚三). Names written in kana
get kanji chosen here. A choice marked (?) has no source in the game.

## 格式

- Speaker labels are the name and a full-width colon: `步美：`. The Japanese opens each line
  with 「 and never closes it; the Chinese has neither.
- Ayumi's thoughts go in full-width parentheses （……）, as in the Japanese.
- Pauses (・・・) become ……, or a single … where the Japanese has a short one.
- Letters, quotes and titles in 『』 become “”; book titles take 《》.
- Use full-width Chinese punctuation: ，。！？：；、“”（）. Use no spaces in Chinese text.
- Numbers as ASCII digits for times, dates and ages: 6分42秒, 凌晨2点到3点, 2月9日, 17岁.
- Drop the Japanese `{n}` line breaks: the game wraps Chinese by itself, between any two
  characters, and keeps closing punctuation off the start of a row. Keep a `{n}` only where
  the layout needs it, such as a heading on its own row.
- Translation-own tags: `{n}` breaks a line, `{c}` clears the window for a new page. Use
  `{c}` only where the Japanese overwrites its own text across long waits, such as the cast
  credits.
- A menu label holds 16 bytes: a Chinese character takes 2, so 8 characters at most.

## 语气 (Voice)

Natural modern Mandarin with the feel of a TV mystery. Short sentences: a window holds three
rows of 18 characters, and the first row also holds the speaker's name. Family and friends use
first names. さん becomes 先生 or 小姐 with a family name where the Japanese is formal, and is
dropped between people close to each other. Officers call Ayumi 橘小姐. Ayumi is warm, quick and
a bit teasing with people she knows, and polite with strangers. Utsugi is calm and kind. Sakuma
is gruff and official. Villagers speak plainly, rural but not comic: no dialect spellings.
Profiles read "17岁。" with Chinese school years (高二).

## 必须逐字保留的线索 (Clues to keep word for word)

- “从偏房出来的脚印先朝大门走，然后又折向正房的门口。”
- Snow began “傍晚” and stopped “凌晨1点过后不久”.
- Estimated time of death: “凌晨2点到3点之间”. The body was found “快到6点的时候”.
